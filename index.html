a lovely day

It was an ordinary day. I cannot quite recall why I went over to Harshal's place, but I know that there was no plan (I was probably locked out of my apartment). It was a few days before graduation and we had work to do – H had to manage the reservation for his family, I had to pick up tickets for our graduation, we had thesis books to submit etc. Wordlessly, we decided we were tagging along. We giggled our way across India Point Park over to Hilton, then walked all the way to Prov Wash, then walked across the river to the Library to work, picked up groceries at Trader Joe's, probably grabbed lunch in between, most definitely had ice cream at the end. Pretty ordinary.

At some point, though, we came across this old couple who, on hearing my loud laugh over one of Harshal's random lame jokes, told us affectionately, 'Always keep each other happy like this'. An endearing comment for a couple, a weird one for friends who never thought to date each other. Clearly they knew before we did.

And now, three years later, we’ve decided to make a promise out of those words. On March 27, 2027, we’d love for you to join us in LA for what we know will be a lovely day!

Formal invite to follow shortly.
